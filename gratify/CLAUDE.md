# App idea

- App that delays the gratification of using certain app. When opening the app itsself, there will be a settings menu where you can choose apps and how long the gratification delay should be.
- When opening the apps you have set, you will be greeted with a screen that says 'Are you sure you want to open this app?' 'You've tried to open this app x times today'and a timer that counts down the seconds until you can open the app with a nice animation.

# Tech stack

- Flutter for cross-platform mobile app development

# Setting

- Clean, minimalistic design with a focus on user experience. Use calming colors and simple animations to make the waiting process more pleasant, but not too pleasant to defeat the purpose of delaying gratification.
- The app should be easy to navigate, with clear instructions and intuitive controls for setting up the delay times and selecting the apps to restrict.

# Development plan

1. Think of what steps have to be taken to build the app and write them down in a list.
2. Create an mvp of the app with basic functionality to test the concept and gather user feedback.
3. Iterate on the design and functionality based on user feedback, adding features such as customizable messages, different delay times for different apps, and a history of app usage.
